Commands

Symphony's music commands.

!play Play a song
!join Join your voice channel
!leave Leave the voice channel
!disconnect Disconnect Symphony
!pause Pause the current track
!resume Resume playback
!skip Skip the current track
!stop Stop playback
!replay Replay the current track
!previous Play the previous track
!nowplaying Show the currently playing track

About Symphony

A music bot built with simplicity in mind.

♡ What is Symphony?

Symphony is a Discord music bot created by Bobby, designed to give Discord communities a simple, smooth, and enjoyable way to listen to music together.

୨୧ What can Symphony do?

Symphony provides music playback and voice-channel controls through simple prefix commands. You can play music, pause and resume playback, skip tracks, replay songs, play the previous track, check what's currently playing, and manage Symphony's voice connection.

✦ Music Controls

Symphony includes commands for playing music, joining and leaving voice channels, pausing and resuming tracks, skipping songs, stopping playback, replaying tracks, returning to the previous track, and viewing the currently playing song.

⌇ Simple Commands

Symphony uses the ! prefix to keep its commands simple and easy to remember. The current command system includes play, join, leave, disconnect, pause, resume, skip, stop, replay, previous, and now playing.

♡ Built by Bobby

Symphony is part of Bobby's journey into software development. It is a personal project built to learn, experiment, improve coding skills, and turn ideas into something real.

୨୧ The Vision

Symphony is designed to stay focused on music instead of becoming overloaded with unrelated features. The goal is to keep the experience clean, straightforward, and easy to use.

Symphony will continue to receive improvements, bug fixes, performance updates, and new music features as development continues.
